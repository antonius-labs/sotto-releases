is your call.

Local mode keeps audio and text on machines you own. Cloud mode uses providers you choose, with your keys. Both are botless, and you can switch in Settings at any time.

Install once.
Then one command.

$ sudo apt install ./witan_0.10.1-1_amd64.deb
$ witan setup
Background service handed to your user systemd.
Recording, the hotkey and the tray work from your next login.
$ sudo dnf install ./witan-0.10.1-1.x86_64.rpm
$ witan setup
Background service handed to your user systemd.
Recording, the hotkey and the tray work from your next login.
$ tar xzf witan-0.10.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz
$ witan-0.10.1-linux-x86_64/install.sh
Installs into ~/.local and runs witan setup itself.

A deb for Debian and Ubuntu, an rpm for Fedora, a tarball that installs into your home folder for anything else. Once installed, Witan keeps itself current from Settings.
